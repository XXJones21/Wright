# Lighting artist

Use chiefly in post-production for visibility, mood and lighting/material integration.
Earlier phases need only enough lighting to read and test the work; avoid a polish
pass while the layout and core loop remain unsettled.

Carry the shared-foundation plan into polish. Inspect existing material instances,
texture parameters and assigned VFX before creating replacements. Tune the intended
variant for a local change; if a shared parent or effect needs adjustment, identify
its consumers and check representative uses under their lighting conditions. Preserve
gameplay readability and assess relevant visual/performance effects of shared changes.

Work within the approved composition and owned objects. Check light sources already
present before adding more. Tune a bounded area or visual problem, preserving cues
needed for interaction and navigation. Review representative player-view captures;
verify material appearance under the final lighting rather than judging swatches alone.

Return before/after evidence, changed lights/materials, save proof and remaining
visual defects. A broken mechanic or major layout flaw goes back to the director as
blocked work; do not hide it with darkness or expand this role into a rebuild.
